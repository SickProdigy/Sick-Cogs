# MTG

MTG is an experimental solo and two-player Magic: The Gathering rules prototype for Red.

Version 0.120.10 supports the original 60-card gameplay pool plus 292 playable Limited Edition Alpha printings: every Alpha printing except the three ante cards. ## Play

Lobby and browsing commands remain directly under `[p]mtg`. Match controls live under `[p]mtg action`; uncommon text fallbacks live under `[p]mtg action special` and are only needed when a button or menu is unavailable.

1. Run `[p]mtg challenge @member`.
2. Both players privately inspect their hands with **View hand**.
3. Each player keeps or mulligans.
4. Use `[p]mtg action play <hand-position> [target]`.
5. Use **Pass / next** or `[p]mtg action pass` for priority and phase progression.
6. Use `[p]mtg action attack <battlefield positions>`.
7. The defender uses `[p]mtg action block <attacker-position:blocker-position>`.

Player targets use a Discord user ID. Permanent targets use `USER_ID:FIELD_POSITION`. Twiddle requires an explicit persisted mode: `tap:USER_ID:FIELD_POSITION` or `untap:USER_ID:FIELD_POSITION`. Healing Salve uses `life:PLAYER_ID`, `prevent:PLAYER_ID`, or `prevent:USER_ID:FIELD_POSITION`. Sacrifice uses `sacrifice:FIELD_POSITION` to choose the creature paid as its additional casting cost. For an X spell, append the chosen value after the target: `[p]mtg action play POSITION TARGET X`; use `-` as the target for Earthquake or Hurricane. The public stack is numbered from top to bottom; use `S:POSITION` for Counterspell or an Elemental Blast counter mode. Run `[p]mtg action graveyard [member]` to list a public graveyard, then use `G:POSITION` for a card in your own graveyard. Animate Dead may target another player's graveyard with `USER_ID:G:POSITION`.
Use `[p]mtg action special mask POSITION X` to activate Illusionary Mask; choose through the private control or `[p]mtg action special maskpick POSITION|decline`. Use `[p]mtg action special hydra POSITION prevent|counter` for Rock Hydra’s paid abilities and `[p]mtg action special hydraorder POSITION counters|shields` to choose its replacement ordering. Use `[p]mtg action special channel [amount]` while Channel is active to pay that much life for colorless mana. Use `[p]mtg action special activate <battlefield-position> [PLAYER_ID|USER_ID:POSITION|S:POSITION]` for a supported non-mana ability. Holy Armor, Firebreathing, and Regeneration use the Aura’s battlefield position and automatically affect its displayed attached creature. Targeted abilities require the player or battlefield target shown by their card text and go onto the public stack before resolving. Use `[p]mtg action mana <battlefield-position> [W|U|B|R|G|C]` to activate a supported mana permanent while you have priority. A color is optional for a single-color source and required for a dual land, Black Lotus, or Celestial Prism. Sol Ring adds two colorless mana; Lotus adds three mana of the chosen color and is sacrificed. Creature tap abilities cannot be used while summoning sick. Mana pools are public, persist through spell and ability resolution, and empty when the current step or phase ends.
After blockers, `[p]mtg action special trample <attacker-battlefield-position> <damage-to-blocker>` may assign more than lethal damage to a trampling attacker’s blocker; without it, the engine assigns lethal and carries the maximum legal excess to the defending player. A creature blocking multiple attackers uses `[p]mtg action special blockdamage BLOCKER_POSITION ATTACKER_POSITION:DAMAGE ...`; list attackers in damage-assignment order and assign exactly the blocker's power. An attacker blocked by multiple creatures uses `[p]mtg action special attackdamage ATTACKER_POSITION BLOCKER_POSITION:DAMAGE ...`; list blockers in damage-assignment order. A resolving False Orders choice uses the public select or `[p]mtg action special orders ATTACKER_POSITION`; use `action special orders decline` to remove its current assignments without making a new block. Pass no positions to attack or block to decline combat. Hands are returned as private ephemeral, numbered image pages with a currently-playable-card selector and card-specific prompts and are never included in the public table. Required combat declarations provide attacker and blocker selectors plus explicit No attacks and No blocks controls. If artwork or rendering is unavailable, the same interaction falls back to private text.

## Solo play

Run `[p]mtg solo [red|green] [easy|normal]` to play against the bot with the selected starter deck. `[p]mtg solo normal` is shorthand for a normal game using the red deck. Easy makes deterministic but intentionally incomplete attacks and blocks; normal evaluates its affordable plays and uses all legal attackers. The bot uses the same engine actions, mana, timing, targeting, priority, and combat rules as a human, and its decisions never inspect the human hand. Solo state and bot decisions recover after reloads. Empty priority and blocking windows on the bot's opening turn advance automatically until the human's first turn. Opening hands provide private Keep hand and Mulligan controls that replace the same ephemeral panel with the resulting hand state. Solo games grant no cards, packs, currency, or other rewards.

## Supported rules

Opening hands and London-style mulligans, a randomized starting player, 20 life, five broad turn phases, one land per turn, colored and generic mana costs with automatic legal land tapping, creatures and summoning sickness, haste on Raging Goblin, a response stack with priority passing before phase changes and combat damage, combat, graveyards, empty-library loss, concessions, and persistent recovery.

This is a learning prototype. The versioned playable catalog in `data/cards.json` remains authoritative for matches, starter decks, and future pack pools. It contains five basics, thirty creatures, thirteen instants, and twelve sorceries. Every playable entry is limited to an engine-supported shape. The separate `data/alpha.json` catalog contains all 295 Limited Edition Alpha printings. Two hundred ninety-two Alpha printings are now engine-playable but are not included in the fixed starters or pack pools. Only Contract from Below, Darkpact, and Demonic Attorney remain reference-only because ante is deliberately excluded. Exile and graveyard choices persist by stable card identity; creature removal, bounce, recovery, and reanimation recheck targets on resolution. Alpha basics and original dual lands carry their actual basic land types and mana options; colored payment searches legal land assignments instead of relying on a greedy tap order. The complete inventory, staged rules plan, promotion gate, and paper-only exceptions are documented in `ALPHA_SUPPORT.md`. Pack generation and ownership remain deliberately unimplemented.

## Card data and artwork

Use `[p]mtg catalog [all|playable|alpha] [page|search]` to open the interactive catalog. Each page has a card selector plus previous/next page controls. Selecting a row opens its full details and artwork; previous/next then walks the filtered catalog alphabetically, while **Up to catalog** returns to the exact page and filter you left. For example, `[p]mtg catalog alpha 2` opens the second Alpha page and `[p]mtg catalog alpha lotus` searches Alpha. Only the member who opened a browser can operate it. Use `[p]mtg card <key or name>` for a direct lookup; `[p]mtg card alpha Black Lotus` forces an Alpha lookup, and exact Alpha printing keys use `lea:NUMBER`. Every detail view labels whether a card is playable or reference-only. Catalog entries store stable Scryfall printing and Oracle identifiers separately from active matches.

Card metadata and images are provided at runtime by [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com). Requests identify this project, use HTTPS and explicit Accept headers, and remain below Scryfall's published API ceiling. Images are decoded and size-checked before entering a private least-recently-used cache limited to 192 files and 128 MiB. Card images are not committed to this repository.

The bundled Alpha metadata was retrieved from the Scryfall `set:lea` printing search. Maintainers can reproduce it with `python mtg/scripts/update_alpha_catalog.py`; the script validates the set count and unique printing identifiers, restricts pagination to Scryfall HTTPS URLs, and paces requests. No Alpha card artwork is bundled.
