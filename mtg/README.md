# MTG

MTG is an experimental solo and two-player Magic: The Gathering rules prototype for Red.

Version 0.60.0 supports the original 60-card gameplay pool plus 205 playable Limited Edition Alpha printings: all 15 textless Alpha creatures, 54 creatures with validated abilities, all 19 Alpha lands, 49 instants and sorceries, 37 enchantments, and thirty-one artifacts. The Aura foundation adds persisted cross-controller attachments, stable target choices and resolution-time fizzling, state-based cleanup, dynamic stat and keyword effects, attached-creature activations, combat restrictions, public attachment rendering, and solo-AI use. Full color protection now prevents matching-color damage, Aura attachment, blocking, and targeting while preserving each Ward on the creature it protects; Black Knight, White Knight, and all five Wards are fully playable. Lord of Atlantis, Zombie Master, and Goblin King now grant live controller-only subtype effects: stacking stat bonuses, islandwalk/swampwalk/mountainwalk, and respondable regeneration that remains independent after activation. Trample now assigns only lethal damage to a blocker and carries excess to the defending player, including correct interactions with existing damage, protection, regeneration, first strike, and blockers that leave combat; War Mammoth is fully playable. Purelace, Thoughtlace, Deathlace, Chaoslace, and Lifelace now change a spell or permanent to exactly one color indefinitely, with live effects on protection, target restrictions, combat, Aura legality, persistence, public rendering, and solo AI. Castle, Crusade, Bad Moon, and Orcish Oriflamme now provide live stacking global creature modifiers scoped by controller, current color, untapped state, and attacking state; state-based cleanup, persistence, public rendering, and solo AI all share those derived stats. Blessing, Fear, and Aspect of Wolf add a respondable attached +1/+1 activation, live black/artifact blocking restrictions, and Forest-scaled power/toughness derived from the Aura controller across persistence, rendering, cleanup, and solo AI. Wild Growth and Mana Flare feed immediate, stacking multi-output mana into manual and automatic payment, while Manabarbs and Psychic Venom create persisted, respondable damage triggers above paid spells and abilities or after non-mana taps. Deathgrip and Lifeforce add respondable activated counters for green and black spells, respectively; their stable stack targets survive source removal and recheck live spell color on resolution. Icy Manipulator, Rod of Ruin, and Jayemdae Tome add reusable paid artifact activations for stable permanent tapping, any-target damage, and card draw; costs are atomic, effects remain independent after source removal, public views expose pending abilities, and solo AI uses the same paths. Celestial Prism adds atomic paid color filtering, while Basalt Monolith produces three colorless mana, skips normal untapping, and uses a respondable paid untap ability; manual actions, persistence, rendering, and AI share those rules. Gauntlet of Might adds stacking live red-creature bonuses and extra red mana from every Mountain, while Lifetap creates persisted respondable life-gain triggers whenever an opponent taps a Forest, including non-mana taps. Ankh of Mishra and Dingus Egg add persisted, respondable damage triggers for land entry and battlefield-to-graveyard land movement across ordinary plays plus targeted and mass destruction. Nevinyrral's Disk enters tapped and adds respondable, regenerable mass destruction for artifacts, creatures, and enchantments that survives source removal and preserves lands. Black Vise, Copper Tablet, and Howling Mine add persisted, respondable upkeep and draw-step triggers with live hand-size damage, APNAP ordering, source-removal independence, and the opening-player draw skip. Meekstone applies a live global power-based untap restriction after temporary cleanup, while Sunglasses of Urza lets actual white mana satisfy red requirements across pooled and automatic payment without changing that mana's identity. Crystal Rod, Iron Star, Ivory Cup, Throne of Bone, and Wooden Sphere create color-matched cast triggers with normal response windows followed by an explicit persisted Pay {1} or Decline decision; payments use real mana sources, survive source removal, render publicly, and work for solo AI. Mana Vault adds explicit three-colorless-mana activation, skips normal untapping, offers a persisted Pay {4} upkeep choice, and conditionally damages its controller at the draw step while tapped. Soul Net adds persisted optional life triggers for ordinary, state-based, simultaneous, and sacrificed creature deaths, excludes regeneration and exile, retains simultaneous last-known sources, and orders multi-controller batches in APNAP order. Jade Statue adds a shared live battlefield-type layer and a persisted combat-only activation that makes it a 3/6 Golem artifact creature until end of combat, with creature targeting, blocking, damage, death triggers, Aura cleanup, rendering, and solo AI all following the animated state. The Hive adds reusable Wasp creation and a persisted non-collectible token model: its 1/1 flying artifact creature tokens obey summoning sickness and combat, trigger creature-death abilities, and cease to exist after leaving the battlefield instead of entering a hand, graveyard, or exile. Conservator adds persisted, stackable next-damage prevention that applies consistently to player damage from spells, abilities, triggers, self-damage, and combat, expires at turn cleanup, renders publicly, and is used by solo AI only for credible pending damage. Fog adds persisted turn-wide combat-damage prevention across first strike, normal damage, unblocked attackers, blockers, and trample while leaving noncombat damage intact; public state and solo AI share the effect and timing. Samite Healer extends next-damage prevention to stable player or creature targets through a centralized creature-damage path covering combat, spells, abilities, mass damage, protection, persistence, cleanup, public rendering, and threat-aware solo AI. Healing Salve adds an explicit persisted choice between giving a target player 3 life and preventing the next 3 damage to a target player or creature, with atomic command validation, protection and fizzle handling, and solo-AI mode selection. Mana Short taps every land controlled by its stable player target through the shared non-mana tap path, clears that player’s floating mana, produces the correct Psychic Venom and Lifetap triggers without Manabarbs, persists on the stack, and is used by solo AI against available opposing mana. Time Walk schedules a persisted extra turn for its controller, orders multiple created turns newest-first, resumes ordinary alternation afterward, runs every normal turn step including the draw step, renders the queue publicly, and is cast by solo AI. Mind Twist and Hypnotic Specter add server-side random discard without exposing private hands: Mind Twist persists its player target and X value, while actual opponent damage from the Specter creates an independent persisted trigger that respects damage prevention and survives source removal. Cockatrice and Thicket Basilisk snapshot non-Wall creatures they block or become blocked by, persist that delayed effect through source removal, resolve damage triggers before entering a visible end-combat response window, and then perform regenerable destruction. Phantasmal Forces and Force of Nature create persisted upkeep payment choices with their printed colored costs; declining sacrifices the Forces or deals eight preventable damage from Force of Nature, including after source removal. Karma, Feedback, Cursed Land, Warp Artifact, and Wanderlust add persisted upkeep damage driven by live Swamp counts or the controller of an enchanted enchantment, land, artifact, or creature; triggers remain independent after source removal and use shared prevention. Wheel of Fortune and Timetwister add private-zone-safe mass redraws, including correct discard-versus-shuffle behavior, Timetwister's self-exclusion, full stack persistence, and simultaneous empty-library loss handling. Drain Life adds black-only X payment and any-target draining whose life gain follows damage prevention and the target's pre-damage life or toughness cap. Spell Blast adds exact-X countering based on a persisted target spell's mana value, including chosen X values and zero-mana spells, with pre-payment and resolution checks. Fastbond permits any number of land plays on its controller's turn and creates a separate preventable damage trigger from each Fastbond for every land after the first; turn counters persist safely, stack triggers survive source removal, and solo AI avoids lethal extra plays. Animate Wall, Holy Armor, Holy Strength, Lance, Flight, Invisibility, Unholy Strength, Weakness, Burrowing, Firebreathing, Regeneration, and Web are fully playable. Death Ward, Jump, Twiddle, Dark Ritual, Tunnel, and Tranquility add targeted regeneration, temporary flying, explicit tap/untap choices, spell-produced mana, non-regenerable Wall destruction, and mass enchantment destruction. Regeneration now uses persisted one-shot shields that replace eligible destruction, tap and heal the permanent, remove it from combat while preserving blocked attackers, expire at cleanup, and respect the explicit Terror, Wrath of God, and Disintegrate prohibitions. Drudge Skeletons, Wall of Bone, Will-o'-the-Wisp, Sedge Troll, Uthden Troll, Wall of Brambles, and Living Wall are fully playable; Sedge Troll also receives its live Swamp bonus. Non-mana activated abilities now use unique persisted stack objects, stable targets, response windows, resolution-time legality checks, tap and mana costs, and summoning-sickness enforcement. Northern Paladin, Prodigal Sorcerer, Royal Assassin, Dwarven Demolition Team, Dwarven Warriors, Orcish Artillery, and Ley Druid add targeted destruction, damage, temporary unblockability, and land untapping. Goblin Balloon Brigade gains persisted temporary flying that affects blocking and flying-sensitive effects, while Dragon Whelp tracks each activation and creates a persisted, respondable end-step sacrifice trigger after the fourth. Frozen Shade, Granite Gargoyle, Shivan Dragon, Wall of Fire, and Wall of Water provide repeatable paid self-pump abilities with asymmetric persisted bonuses, cleanup, combat-aware AI use, and a shared activation command. The X-spell foundation provides user-selected values, scalable effects, and Disintegrate’s turn-long exile replacement. Counterspell and the Elemental Blasts provide stable stack interaction. Nightmare, Plague Rats, and Keldon Warlord use live battlefield-derived power and toughness across combat, state-based actions, public rendering, and solo AI. Birds of Paradise and Llanowar Elves provide creature mana abilities with summoning-sickness enforcement. Zone actions include stable graveyard targeting, hand return, reanimation, restricted/mass removal, and persisted exile. Public embeds/playmats show graveyard and exile counts, while bounded public commands expose legal choices without revealing private zones. The solo AI shares the same casting, target, mana, and sickness rules. The full Alpha catalog remains 295 printings representing 290 distinct names. The two fixed 60-card beginner decks and 60-card pack pool remain unchanged.

## Play

1. Run `[p]mtg challenge @member`.
2. Both players privately inspect their hands with **View hand**.
3. Each player keeps or mulligans.
4. Use `[p]mtg play <hand-position> [target]`.
5. Use **Pass / next** or `[p]mtg pass` for priority and phase progression.
6. Use `[p]mtg attack <battlefield positions>`.
7. The defender uses `[p]mtg block <attacker-position:blocker-position>`.

Player targets use a Discord user ID. Permanent targets use `USER_ID:FIELD_POSITION`. Twiddle requires an explicit persisted mode: `tap:USER_ID:FIELD_POSITION` or `untap:USER_ID:FIELD_POSITION`. Healing Salve uses `life:PLAYER_ID`, `prevent:PLAYER_ID`, or `prevent:USER_ID:FIELD_POSITION`. For an X spell, append the chosen value after the target: `[p]mtg play POSITION TARGET X`; use `-` as the target for Earthquake or Hurricane. The public stack is numbered from top to bottom; use `S:POSITION` for Counterspell or an Elemental Blast counter mode. Run `[p]mtg graveyard [member]` to list a public graveyard, then use `G:POSITION` for a card in your own graveyard.
Use `[p]mtg activate <battlefield-position> [PLAYER_ID|USER_ID:POSITION|S:POSITION]` for a supported non-mana ability. Holy Armor, Firebreathing, and Regeneration use the Aura’s battlefield position and automatically affect its displayed attached creature. Targeted abilities require the player or battlefield target shown by their card text and go onto the public stack before resolving. Use `[p]mtg mana <battlefield-position> [W|U|B|R|G|C]` to activate a supported mana permanent while you have priority. A color is optional for a single-color source and required for a dual land, Black Lotus, or Celestial Prism. Sol Ring adds two colorless mana; Lotus adds three mana of the chosen color and is sacrificed. Creature tap abilities cannot be used while summoning sick. Mana pools are public, persist through spell and ability resolution, and empty when the current step or phase ends.
After blockers, `[p]mtg trample <attacker-battlefield-position> <damage-to-blocker>` may assign more than lethal damage to a trampling attacker’s blocker; without it, the engine assigns lethal and carries the maximum legal excess to the defending player. Pass no positions to attack or block to decline combat. Hands are returned as private ephemeral, numbered image pages and are never included in the public table. If artwork or rendering is unavailable, the same interaction falls back to private text.

## Solo play

Run `[p]mtg solo [red|green] [easy|normal]` to play against the bot with the selected starter deck. `[p]mtg solo normal` is shorthand for a normal game using the red deck. Easy makes deterministic but intentionally incomplete attacks and blocks; normal evaluates its affordable plays and uses all legal attackers. The bot uses the same engine actions, mana, timing, targeting, priority, and combat rules as a human, and its decisions never inspect the human hand. Solo state and bot decisions recover after reloads. Solo games grant no cards, packs, currency, or other rewards.

## Supported rules

Opening hands and London-style mulligans, a randomized starting player, 20 life, five broad turn phases, one land per turn, colored and generic mana costs with automatic legal land tapping, creatures and summoning sickness, haste on Raging Goblin, a response stack with priority passing before phase changes and combat damage, combat, graveyards, empty-library loss, concessions, and persistent recovery.

This is a learning prototype. The versioned playable catalog in `data/cards.json` remains authoritative for matches, starter decks, and future pack pools. It contains five basics, thirty creatures, thirteen instants, and twelve sorceries. Every playable entry is limited to an engine-supported shape. The separate `data/alpha.json` catalog contains all 295 Limited Edition Alpha printings. Two hundred five Alpha printings are now engine-playable but are not included in the fixed starters or pack pools: 69 creatures, all 19 lands, 49 instants and sorceries, 37 enchantments, and thirty-one artifacts. The remaining 90 printings stay reference-only. Exile and graveyard choices persist by stable card identity; creature removal, bounce, recovery, and reanimation recheck targets on resolution. Alpha basics and original dual lands carry their actual basic land types and mana options; colored payment searches legal land assignments instead of relying on a greedy tap order. The complete inventory, staged rules plan, promotion gate, and paper-only exceptions are documented in `ALPHA_SUPPORT.md`. Pack generation and ownership remain deliberately unimplemented.

## Card data and artwork

Use `[p]mtg catalog [all|playable|alpha] [page|search]` to open the interactive catalog. Each page has a card selector plus previous/next page controls. Selecting a row opens its full details and artwork; previous/next then walks the filtered catalog alphabetically, while **Up to catalog** returns to the exact page and filter you left. For example, `[p]mtg catalog alpha 2` opens the second Alpha page and `[p]mtg catalog alpha lotus` searches Alpha. Only the member who opened a browser can operate it. Use `[p]mtg card <key or name>` for a direct lookup; `[p]mtg card alpha Black Lotus` forces an Alpha lookup, and exact Alpha printing keys use `lea:NUMBER`. Every detail view labels whether a card is playable or reference-only. Catalog entries store stable Scryfall printing and Oracle identifiers separately from active matches.

Card metadata and images are provided at runtime by [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com). Requests identify this project, use HTTPS and explicit Accept headers, and remain below Scryfall's published API ceiling. Images are decoded and size-checked before entering a private least-recently-used cache limited to 192 files and 128 MiB. Card images are not committed to this repository.

The bundled Alpha metadata was retrieved from the Scryfall `set:lea` printing search. Maintainers can reproduce it with `python mtg/scripts/update_alpha_catalog.py`; the script validates the set count and unique printing identifiers, restricts pagination to Scryfall HTTPS URLs, and paces requests. No Alpha card artwork is bundled.
